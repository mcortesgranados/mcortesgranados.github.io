Technical detail

  • 📚 CQRS — separated write model from read models/projections, optimised query paths (denormalised views, materialised reads) and kept the command side transactional and validated.
  • 🗃️ Event Sourcing — append-only event logs as the source of truth with projections rebuilt on demand; useful for audit-heavy domains (financial movements, approvals, workflow history) where the full history matters.
  • 📤 Transactional Outbox — atomic "state + event" writes with a relay/publisher, guaranteeing reliable publication to Kafka/RabbitMQ/Service Bus without 2PC; combined with inbox/dedup tables on the consumer side.
  • 🔄 Saga — choreographed and orchestrated long-running transactions with compensating actions for order/payment/provisioning-style flows, plus timeouts and manual-intervention escape hatches.
  • ⚡ Event-driven architecture — topics/queues with clear schemas, versioning and ownership; producers/consumers decoupled, replayable and observable.
  • 🔁 Idempotency — idempotency keys, deduplication stores, conditional writes and unique constraints so at-least-once delivery produces exactly-once business effects.
  • 🛡️ Retry / Circuit Breaker — Polly-style policies with exponential backoff and jitter, circuit breakers, bulkheads, timeouts and fallbacks so a failing dependency degrades gracefully instead of cascading.

🧠 I also apply the discipline around them: pattern choice justified in an ADR, failure paths tested (chaos-style and integration tests), and metrics/alerting that reveal backlog, retries and compensation events before customers notice.
